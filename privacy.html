Cent Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy describes how Cent Bot ("the app"), a personal desktop application operated by YOUR_NAME ("I", "me"), handles information, including data obtained from Google APIs. Contact: EMAIL.

1. Where your data lives

Cent Bot runs on your own computer. I do not operate any server that receives, stores or processes your data, and I do not have access to your conversations, files, Google data or credentials. Profiles, conversations, settings and activity logs are stored locally on your device. Access tokens and API keys are encrypted using your operating system's secure storage.

2. Google user data the app can access

Only if you connect your Google account, and only for the permissions (scopes) you approve on Google's consent screen, the app can access:

  • Gmail: read and search messages and threads, manage labels, create and edit drafts, and send a message only after you approve it.
  • Google Calendar: view events and create or update events at your request.
  • Google Drive: search and read files, and create files, at your request.
  • Basic profile: your email address and name, to label the connected account.

3. How Google user data is used

The app uses this data only to perform the tasks you ask your assistants to do, such as summarizing an email, drafting a reply or finding a free time slot. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with me or any third party for my own purposes. No humans read your Google data: it is processed on your computer.

When you ask an assistant to work with content (for example, to summarize an email), that content is included in the request sent to the AI model provider that you selected in the app (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter or a local model). That transfer happens only to provide the feature you requested, and is governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy. If you prefer that no content leaves your computer, use a local model.

4. Storage and retention

Google tokens and cached results are kept on your device until you remove the connected account or delete the profile. Removing an account in the app revokes its token with Google. Deleting the application's data folder removes all locally stored information.

5. Your controls

  • Disconnect any account at any time in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Turn individual tools on or off, and require approval for any action.
  • Review the local activity log of everything the assistants did.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Cent Bot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: EMAIL.
