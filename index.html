$Cent Bot

Cent Bot is a personal desktop application that gives you AI assistants ("bots"). Each bot has a role, its own conversation and its own workspace, and it can help you with email, calendar, files and everyday tasks on your own computer.

What the app does

  • Chat with several assistants, each with its own role and memory.
  • With your explicit permission, an assistant can read and search your Gmail, prepare email drafts, view and create Google Calendar events, and create or read files in Google Drive.
  • Every action that sends, changes or deletes something asks for your approval first, and every action is written to a local activity log you can review.
  • Your data stays on your computer. The app has no server of its own.

Google account access

Cent Bot connects to your Google account only when you choose to, using Google's standard sign-in. You can remove access at any time in the app (Connect apps) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See the Privacy Policy for exactly how Google user data is accessed, used and stored.

Status

Cent Bot is a personal project under active development. It is used by its author and is not offered as a commercial service.
